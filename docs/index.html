METHODOLOGY

How the conviction score is built

Conviction is a single 0–10 number answering one question: is this worth owning, right now, on a risk-adjusted basis? It is deterministic — the same dated fundamentals snapshot always yields the same score — and it reads all five signals, not just a headline growth rate.

The core idea: reward × safety, geometric

Every conviction score is the geometric mean of a reward term and a safety term:

CONV = √( REWARD × SAFETY )

The geometric form is deliberate. With a plain average, a superb business at a terrible entry price could hide its risk behind its strong side. Multiplying instead means both terms have to be decent — a near-zero on either axis drags the whole score down. That is the mathematical way of saying “the weakest layer is the real risk.”

CYCLE / CATALYST names

Names you buy for a move — small, cheap, accelerating. Reward is driven by growth; safety leans on the 8-point entry screen.

REWARD = 0.50·F + 0.50·GROWTH
SAFETY = 0.35·V + 0.25·8PT + 0.20·C + 0.20·bind
  • bind = the value of the weakest of F/V/C — counted a second time so the binding constraint gets extra weight.
  • [PEAK?] names are cut ×0.85 (a low PEG on peak-cycle earnings is fake-cheap).
DCA compounders

Names you buy on a schedule regardless of price. Growth and the 8-point screen are dropped (they penalise the size and extension a proven compounder is allowed to have); price discipline enters via richness instead.

REWARD = 0.50·QUALITY + 0.50·F
SAFETY = 0.45·V + 0.35·(1−RICHNESS) + 0.20·bind
  • RICHNESS is the DCA price gate (0 = cheap … 1 = stretched): a rich name is slowed, never skipped.
  • No [PEAK?] haircut — a peak-cycle flag is meaningless for a buy-forever name.

In both variants a name with thin data (coverage < 75%) has its score scaled down by its coverage — a number earned on guesses is trusted less. The dashboard picks the right variant per name automatically from its strategy tag.

The three layers: F / V / C

Under the score sit three layers, each 0–10 where higher = safer. They regroup the same underlying fundamentals by the force each represents, so the layers can never contradict the numbers they are built from. The weakest layer is the binding constraint — the dominant risk — and is highlighted on every card.

F · Fundamentals

Business quality, momentum-neutral. Net margin, forward revenue & EPS growth, free-cash-flow status, margin trajectory, gross-margin level and revenue-growth consistency — blended.

V · Valuation

How much of the business is already in the price. PEG first (P/S-vs-growth as a fallback for loss-makers), plus how far the chart sits above its 200-day average — extension is paid-up optimism.

C · Cycle

Position in the wave and crowding. Built from cycle tags plus how extended the chart is and whether the name sits on a real supply bottleneck — so it is never blank.
